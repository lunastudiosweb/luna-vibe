# LUNA VIBE

**Music. Without the noise.**

Luna Vibe is a music platform developed by **LUNA Studios** to help listeners discover and enjoy music without advertisements or interruptions.

Luna Vibe is a LUNA Studios branch focused on independent music, artist submissions, and a simple listening experience.

## Features

- **Ad-free listening** — Enjoy music without advertisements.
- **Independent music** — Discover music from independent artists and partnered studios.
- **Simple interface** — A clean, minimal design focused on music.
- **Audio player** — Listen to tracks directly on the website.
- **Automatic playback** — Continue listening when a track finishes.
- **Volume control** — Adjust playback volume.
- **Loop playback** — Repeat tracks when desired.
- **Song pages** — View individual tracks and their information.
- **Artist submissions** — Artists can submit music for consideration.

## Submitting Music

Artists interested in submitting music should use the submission instructions on the website.

**Submission requirements:**
- You must own the rights to the music you submit.
- Submissions are subject to review.
- Submitting a track does not guarantee publication.
